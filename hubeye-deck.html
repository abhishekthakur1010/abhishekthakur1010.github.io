The problem

A system built to watch, not to act.

  • Read-only tiles across 14 tabs: counts without context.
  • Tables to read, not a picture to see.
  • Insight lived outside the tool, in spreadsheets and on paper.

The question
How might we help hub managers spot and act on issues in real time, without leaving the dashboard?
Research on the floor

Designed on the hub floor, not at a desk.

What we learned

Three insights shaped the design.

Insight 1

Location is the first question

Supervisors don't ask "which row in the table". They ask "which dock" and walk there.

Insight 2

Decisions happen on the move

No time to read. The screen must make sense in a glance from across the room.

Insight 3

The tool lost to the spreadsheet

Teams rebuilt the picture in sheets because HubEye couldn't say what to fix.

The solution

The hub, mapped 1:1. Problems show up where they happen.

eye.delhivery.com/mid-mile/hub · Tauru Gateway
Visual strategy

Grey or red. Nothing else.

A two-state system so a supervisor scanning 300+ sectors and docks finds every critical zone in seconds. No in-between states to decode.

GreyNormal · no action
RedAlert · act now
Detail on click

Click any sector. Get the why.

Hub vitals

Every hub metric, one glance away.

  • Persistent snapshot beside the map: shipments, yard, docks, manpower, compliance.
  • Red only when it matters, like a 12% sorter rejection rate.
  • Collapses when the floor needs the full screen.
  • Quick links to CT, cost and manpower dashboards.
Plan ahead · see the floor

From "what's wrong now" to "what's coming next".

Expected Yard Waiting forecasts vehicles against planned docks, hour by hour. Floor Images puts live dock cameras one click from a red dock.
