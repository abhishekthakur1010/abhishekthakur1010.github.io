01 Context

The Last Mile is where the brand promise is kept or broken.

Delhivery is India's largest logistics company — parcel delivery, warehousing, freight, and cross-border, serving 18,000+ pin codes since 2011. I led design for its fastest-moving layer: the Express Last Mile, spanning dispatch, delivery, partner onboarding, payments, and field engagement.

02 Problem

One promise, five broken tools.

Field visits, shadowing, and system audits surfaced friction at every step of the Last Mile — hurting both operations and experience.

1

FE App

Outdated UI, unclear task flows, and poor route visibility slowed agents and hurt delivery success.

2

Partner onboarding

Manual, telecaller-led onboarding was error-prone and drove partner drop-off. No self-serve meant no scale.

3

Payments

~85% of COD was cash — a safety and reconciliation risk. QR was buried and failed often on weak networks.

4

Engagement

Field execs had no motivation beyond payouts — no way to reward performance or recognize progress.

5

Visibility

The LM Eye dashboard was hard to navigate with no parcel-level real-time view, so DC teams couldn't act fast.

!

…and a process problem

A 12:2 PM-to-designer ratio and 5-day sprints kept the team reacting, not planning.

03 Solution & decisions

Rebuilt for speed, clarity, and fewer errors.

Grounded in on-ground immersion — I shadowed FEs and delivered parcels myself — every decision optimized for one thing: faster, error-free action for people working on their feet.

FE App 2.0 · Landing

A field-first home screen

Rebranded to a high-contrast black/red palette for outdoor readability, cut clutter, and surfaced the day's work first — with "Profile Strength" nudges to pull agents through onboarding.

Decision: prioritize today's tasks over navigation depth.
FE App 2.0 · Shipment listing

Sorted for how the eye moves

Applied proximity and F/Z gaze patterns, added a map view, moved key tabs to a bottom nav, and pushed urgent stops to the top so agents plan routes at a glance.

Decision: let priority — not chronology — drive the list order.
Digital Payments 2.0

QR-first, cash-last

Made QR the always-visible default, added clear payment feedback and proof capture, and nudged agents away from cash at the moment of collection.

Result: QR adoption rose from ~21% to ~32%, saving ~₹2.5L/day in cash handling.
Partner App 2.0 · Lead Gen

Self-serve onboarding that scales

Replaced telecaller onboarding with an interactive self-serve flow — a clear 3-step path (Work Details → Documents → First Order), refer-and-earn, and forms paired with document mockups to cut errors.

Decision: recognition over recall — show, don't ask.
Delhivery Premier League

Turned performance into a game

A gamified performance layer with badges, live ranks, and real prizes (MacBook, iPhone) surfaced up front — reinforced with a bold black/red identity to build morale and healthy competition.

Decision: make progress visible and rewarding, not just measured.
