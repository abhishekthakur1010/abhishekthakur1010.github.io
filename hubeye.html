09Detail on click

Click any sector. Get the why.

Red says what is wrong. Green says what is fine.

10Hub vitals

Every hub metric, one glance away.

One panel beside the map with every number that matters.

  • Collapses when the floor needs the full screen.
  • Turns red only when a number needs action, like a 12% sorter rejection rate.
  • Quick links jump to the CT, cost and manpower dashboards, or download pending putaway.
