Breaking it down

Coming from a humble base, I always aimed higher. Cracking tier-1 exams like IIT and UPSC was my way to move forward and unlock new opportunities. I got into UX design early during my B.Tech in IT at IIIT Allahabad, driven by a deep curiosity about people, systems and creativity. Over time, I realized that both design and UPSC share a common purpose - understanding people and solving real problems. That purpose drew me to both paths and shaped me into someone who is empathetic, structured, and grounded.

Working with me feels easy and positive. I try to bring a friendly, happy energy that creates harmony in the team, because good vibes lead to better work. I believe it’s not just about getting the job done, but also about giving everyone a chance to grow, contribute, and feel heard. I support both my team and seniors with the same spirit of collaboration and respect.

Both discipline shaped me in different ways. IIIT showed me the power of persistence and how repetition can build mastery. UPSC, on the other hand, taught me patience, empathy, and the ability to see a problem from multiple perspectives. It also helped me develop deep focus and the skill to simplify complex ideas, something that stays with me in every project I work on.

I’ve worked in logistics, e-commerce, food tech, telecom, edtech, and health. Solving real-world, everyday problems excites me, especially where design can create clarity in complex systems.

The bigger picture. Even small progress matters. I remind myself why I started and stay connected to people and purpose.

Outside work, I stay active through cycling, running, and workouts - it helps me stay focused and clear-headed. One of my proudest moments was cycling from Gurgaon to Dehradun and back on my TREK MTB X-Caliber, completing the round trip in 23 hours (with a 5-hour rest). I also find joy in traveling, volunteering with RHA and the CRY Foundation, and spending time with dogs and in nature. These moments keep me grounded and always offer fresh perspectives.

I follow a flexible yet structured process: Understand > Research > Sketch > Design > Test > Iterate. While the exact flow may vary by problem or company, this core approach keeps me user-focused and outcome-driven.

I prioritize based on value and feasibility. I always ask: “Why are we doing this, and what’s the user getting out of it?” This helps ensure that every design decision serves both user needs and business goals.

While shopping online, I noticed the most frustrating moment is when users need help but feel unheard or stuck in long FAQ lists. At JioMart, I redesigned the Help & Support flow into a conversational, guided experience with real-time ticket visibility. It felt more human, reduced confusion and made users feel genuinely supported - turning a pain point into a trust-building moment.

I keep it honest and kind. Focus on the “why” behind design choices. I welcome feedback as a chance to improve.

How I Design?
The ‘Reality to Rollout' Framework
" A clear process to turn a spark of an idea into a fully designed product, combining user empathy with product clarity
Taking it step-by-step
Understand & Align
I start by sitting down with you to explore the problem or idea. Through workshops and conversations, I align on goals and expectations
Talk to Users
Next, I conduct interviews and usability tests to hear directly from real users. I observe, learn and collect insights that shape our direction
Define the Blueprint
With clarity in place together, we'll define clear user flows and detailed requirements, ensuring alignment and clarity
Prototyping & User Testing
Next is head down mode. It’s time to bring your idea to life. I'll build interactive prototypes and validate designs through user testing and iterative feedback
Plan & Handoff
With everything tested and approved, I collaborate with dev teams to build a clear, actionable roadmap for a smooth launch
Put simply:
I move from insight to execution—blending grounded UX methods with smart AI acceleration to ship confidently
