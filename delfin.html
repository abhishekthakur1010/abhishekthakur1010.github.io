02Context

Every trip runs through a toll. Every toll needs a FASTag.

Delhivery already works with thousands of truck owners. DelFin turns that relationship into everyday services, starting with FASTag and GPS, and built so fuel, insurance and credit can follow.

Fleet ownerRuns one or many trucks. Buys tags, tops up, tracks the fleet.
Owner-driverDrives their own truck. Needs it fast, on the phone, between trips.

06Key decisions

Four decisions that made it feel simple.

Decision 1

Sell the benefit before asking for anything.

Three swipeable cards say what owners get: lower charges, no toll surprises, cashback. Then a phone number, then a role. Nothing else until it's needed.

Why: partners were signing up without knowing what DelFin was for.

Decision 2

Home shows what needs attention, not what's for sale.

Active counts on each service, then pending actions ("FASTag activation pending · 2 trucks"), then the wallet. New users see the buy cards; returning users see their trucks.

Why: owners came back to check status, not to browse.

Decision 3

Progress you can see, and pick up later.

Each truck gets a tracker: six steps for FASTag, four for GPS, with the current step marked. A day-long KYC or installation resumes where it stopped.

Why: handover and installation happen in the field, often hours later.

Decision 4 · iterated

When money is involved, say exactly what happens.

Cancelling an activation first said "the amount will be refunded". The next version names the amount and where it goes, in a highlighted line: ₹599 back to your DelFin wallet.

Why: vague refund copy made owners call support to confirm.
