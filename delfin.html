Context

Fleet ownerMany trucks, tracks the fleet

Problem

Buying a FASTag was all offline.

  1. Call salesNo way to start on your own
  2. Paper KYCDocuments sent offline
  3. Wait for daysNo idea where the order is
  4. Fail at the tollLow balance or blacklisted tag, found too late

Fleet owner
Two banks, two wallet models. The owner only sees my truck, my balance, what needs attention.

Journey

Install to active tag, in one sitting.

Decisions

Four calls that kept it simple.

Decision 1

Sell the benefit before asking for anything.

Benefits first, then a phone number, then a role.

Decision 2

Home shows what needs attention, not what's for sale.

Pending actions first, the shop second.

Decision 3

Progress you can see, and pick up later.

Every truck gets a tracker it can resume.

Decision 4 · iterated

When money is involved, say exactly what happens.

Name the amount and where it goes.

After buying

No support call needed.
